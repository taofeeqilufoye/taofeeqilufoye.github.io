# Building better ways to work.

My background brings together frontline operations, avionics engineering, and research and development. Across these roles, I have worked on process improvement, locally developed engineering solutions, digital tools, and technical training.

At Amazon, I designed and piloted an AI-enabled shift execution tool and automated parts of the shift review process. In the Nigerian Air Force, I led engineering and R&D initiatives that included aircraft modification, locally produced test equipment, and technician development. Earlier roles at SBAY Global Services and Ericsson covered infrastructure delivery, procurement, and network performance.

## Areas of experience

- **Operations improvement:** labor planning, shift execution, sortation, and process redesign.
- **Engineering and R&D:** avionics, aircraft support, technical problem-solving, and equipment development.
- **Digital enablement:** practical tools and collaborative workflows that reduce manual work.
- **Team development:** technical apprenticeship, training, and cross-functional coordination.

These areas reflect the roles and work described in my experience; they are not a separate list of credentials.
